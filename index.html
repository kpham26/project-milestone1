Introduction

Cybersecurity remains a pillar in the modern tech world. With the evolving need for computers, smartphones, and internet connectivity, the need for data protection from cyber attacks is vital. Cyber security focuses on preventing unauthorized use, detecting potential threats, and responding to attacks that could affect individuals, organizations, or governments.

This site explores the main elements of cybersecurity: what we typically worry about online, how we protect our systems, and what concepts help us understand it all in context. It’s intended to provide a general understanding of how security plays a role in our lives online.

Key Terms

  • Cybersecurity: Protecting systems and data from attacks
  • Malware: Harmful software designed to damage systems
  • Phishing: Fake messages used to steal information
  • Encryption: Protecting data by converting it into code
  • Firewall: A system that blocks unauthorized access
